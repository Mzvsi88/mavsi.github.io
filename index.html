04 — MY STORY

The Journey

Every journey has a beginning. This is part of mine.

LAGOS, NIGERIA · OCTOBER 12

My story is still being written.

I was born in Lagos, Nigeria, on October 12. In 2019, family issues led to my relocation to Onitsha, Anambra State.

Moving to a different place became part of my life journey. My experiences continue to shape the person I am and the stories I want to tell through my creative work.

Today, as Mavsi The Creator, I'm developing my craft in cinematography, filmmaking and visual storytelling. I want my work to capture meaningful moments, express emotions and give people stories they can connect with.

This is my journey. This is my vision. And this is only the beginning.
