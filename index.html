03 — ABOUT MAVSI
THE PERSON BEHIND THE CAMERA

More than just a camera.

I'm Emmanuel Chigozirim, professionally known as Mavsi. I'm a 22-year-old Nigerian cinematographer, director and visual storyteller from Delta State, Nigeria.

Through film and photography, I explore creativity, culture, people and the stories that often go unnoticed. Every frame is created with intention — from lighting and composition to the smallest detail — with the goal of making visuals that don't just look good, but make people feel something.
